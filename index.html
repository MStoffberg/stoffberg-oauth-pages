Home Authentication

This service provides secure sign-in for privately hosted applications and services operated on the Stoffberg homelab.

What this service does

Authentik is used as the identity provider for approved homelab users. Google Sign-In may be offered as an authentication method to verify a user's identity before access is granted to supported services.

Who can use it

Access is limited to invited and authorized users. This is not a public consumer service and does not provide open account registration.
